      The game

      How to play
      1. Build a course. Start at a doorway, weave round chairs, under the table, round the sofa, back to the start.
      2. Run your leg holding your phone like a full glass. Spill and it's gone, and the glass has a slow leak, so don't dawdle.
      3. Pour it on. Back at the start, hold the button and tip your phone over the next runner's phone. They catch it and go.
      4. Make mischief. Everyone not holding a glass throws quakes, ice cubes, fizz, frost and swells at the other team's runner, or puts a lid on their own.
      5. Most drink delivered wins. Each leg's glass is sized to what's left, so it always starts brim-full.
